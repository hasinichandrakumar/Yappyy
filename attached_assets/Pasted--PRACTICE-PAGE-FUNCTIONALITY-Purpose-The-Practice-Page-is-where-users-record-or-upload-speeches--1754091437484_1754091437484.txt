🗣️ PRACTICE PAGE FUNCTIONALITY
Purpose:
The Practice Page is where users record or upload speeches, rehearse presentations, or complete short speaking drills based on prompts (e.g. elevator pitch, debate opener, mock interview, etc.).
Core Features:
🎤 Record: Start/stop video/audio recording using the webcam/mic.
📄 Upload: Option to upload existing speech video or audio.
💡 Prompt Generator: Users can select a prompt or speaking scenario (e.g. business pitch, debate, TED-style talk).
⏱️ Timer: Live countdown to practice time limits.
🔁 Transcript & Filler Word Counter (Live): As the user speaks, a rough transcript and filler word counter appear in real-time (with post-processing accuracy later on).
🚀 "Submit for Analysis" Button: Once done, users submit their speech for detailed AI feedback.
📊 ANALYSIS PAGE FUNCTIONALITY
Purpose:
This is where users get detailed, intelligent feedback on their practice attempt. The goal is to simulate the kind of actionable, specific feedback they’d get from a human coach, but better — backed by AI, visuals, and metrics.
Key Feedback Components:
Transcript
Auto-generated full transcript
Filler words, repeated words, long pauses, and unclear segments highlighted
Verbal Feedback Metrics
📈 Speaking Rate (WPM)
⏸️ Average Pause Length
🔁 Filler Word Count + Specific Words ("um", "like", etc.)
🔄 Word Repetition Tracker
🔊 Volume Analysis (Too soft/loud?)
🎵 Pitch Variation (monotone vs expressive)
Non-Verbal Feedback Metrics (if video input is provided)
👀 Eye Contact (measured through gaze direction and focus)
😐 Facial Expression Tone (neutral, smiling, overly tense, etc.)
🤲 Gesture Frequency & Type (open hands, pointing, etc.)
🧍‍♂️ Posture Movement & Stability
🚶 Walking Pattern (if any)
Personalized Tips
AI-generated suggestions like:
"Try slowing down during key points,"
"Vary your pitch more to maintain attention,"
"Good use of eye contact — consider using more gestures to emphasize points."
Progress Tracking (if user has account history)
Comparison with past attempts (e.g., "25% fewer filler words than last time")
Visual graphs showing improvement over time
